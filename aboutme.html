Issac Tiu

Issac Tiu is a digital artist and game designer specializing in low poly and non-photorealistic graphics across both 2D and 3D medias.

With a diverse experience across many aspects of video games, Issac can model, edit, texture, program and prototype with the various tools he has learned both professionally and independently.

He has a combined bachelor’s degree in computer science and new media from the University of Lethbridge and a certificate in game design from BYJU’s Future School (formerly WhiteHat Jr).

Such art style can be seen in many of his projects including photographic edits, models and websites created throughout his education.
